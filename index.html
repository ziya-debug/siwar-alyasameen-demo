Welcome to Siwar Al Yasameen

A Celebration of Jordanian Hospitality & Culinary Excellence

At Siwar Al Yasameen, we invite you to discover a dining experience that honors the rich traditions of Jordanian hospitality while embracing the refinement of modern culinary artistry. Nestled along Jordan Street in the heart of Amman, our restaurant has become a cherished destination for those who seek not just a meal, but a moment of elegance.

From our sun-drenched terrace with sweeping views to our warmly appointed indoor dining spaces, every corner of Siwar Al Yasameen reflects our commitment to excellence. Our chefs work with the finest ingredients—premium Wagyu beef, hand-picked seasonal vegetables, and aromatic spices—to create dishes that speak to both heritage and innovation.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone with family, hosting colleagues, or simply savoring an afternoon with friends, we welcome you to experience the bracelet of jasmine that has made us one of Amman's most beloved dining destinations.

Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence on Jordan Street

The name Siwar Al Yasameen—"Bracelet of Jasmine"—evokes an image both delicate and enduring, much like the restaurant itself. Like the intertwined blossoms of jasmine that form an elegant bracelet, our establishment weaves together the threads of tradition, quality, and warm hospitality into an experience that has graced the Amman dining scene with distinction.

Since our doors first opened along Jordan Street, near Abu Nuseir and just before Amman Arab University, we have been guided by a singular vision: to create a space where the essence of Jordanian culinary heritage meets the sophistication of contemporary dining. The scent of jasmine, which perfumes our gardens and inspires our name, serves as a constant reminder of the care and attention we bring to every detail.

"At Siwar Al Yasameen, we believe that dining is about more than sustenance—it's about creating memories that linger long after the last bite, conversations that flow as freely as our aromatic coffee, and moments that bring families and friends closer together."

Our location was chosen with intention. Perched to offer sweeping views of Amman's ever-evolving landscape, our terrace has become a destination in itself—a place where the city unfolds before you as you dine. Whether you choose the airy elegance of our terrace or the intimate warmth of our indoor dining rooms, you'll find spaces designed for celebration, reflection, and connection.

The trust of over 2,700 guests who have shared their experiences and awarded us a 4.5-star rating speaks to the consistency of our commitment. From our professional staff who anticipate your every need to our chefs who treat each dish as a work of art, every member of the Siwar Al Yasameen family is dedicated to exceeding expectations.

We take pride in our extensive menu, which honors both time-tested recipes and innovative creations. Our kitchen works exclusively with premium ingredients—from the Wagyu beef that elevates our shawerma and burgers to the fresh, locally-sourced produce that gives life to our salads. Each dish tells a story, whether it's the comforting nostalgia of our Shish Barak or the creative flair of our signature Al Yasameen Cloud dessert.

Today, Siwar Al Yasameen stands as more than a restaurant—it is a landmark, a gathering place, and a testament to what happens when passion, quality, and hospitality intertwine. We invite you to become part of our story, to add your own memories to the bracelet we continue to weave together, one unforgettable meal at a time.
